Camera

On first start the browser will ask for camera permission, and the segmentation model (a few MB) will download once and cache in your browser. Once permission is granted the dropdown lists your available cameras — picking a different one restarts the feed on that camera.

Second Camera (optional)

Off. A second webcam can view the scene at the same time, with the same outline and name recognition — it's a viewer only, though, and doesn't drive the Learn/Record controls below, which stay tied to the first camera.

Camera & Refresh Settings

30 fps
6 / sec

Camera FPS asks your webcam to capture at that rate (actual rate depends on your camera/browser). Refresh rate controls how often the outline/gesture detection re-runs each second — lower it on slower computers, raise it for smoother tracking. "Accurate" body outline uses a much higher-quality segmentation model (~90MB one-time download, cached after) with cleaner edges, at the cost of a slower first load and more GPU use.

People

Have just that one person stand alone in frame, type their name above, then click "Learn This Person". Register as many people as you like — each gets their own outline color. To improve someone already registered, type their exact name and click "Add More Samples" instead of re-learning from scratch. For the best results, click "Record Training Photos" and walk around — turn, step closer, then step farther back — for a while; it keeps capturing photos until you click "Stop Recording", then blends them in. Recognition is also forgiving while you move: once it's confident who you are, it'll keep tracking you through a few uncertain frames (walking, turning, being farther away) instead of needing a perfect match every single frame.

Registered people are live on the page as window.MirrorMePeople (e.g. window.MirrorMePeople["Rayden"] is true/false each frame, and window.MirrorMePeople._visible lists everyone currently matched), plus a mirrorme:person event on window with { name, active, similarity } — so you can assign different values or behavior per recognized person, including when two specific people are both in frame at once.

Hand Gesture Training

Hold one hand clearly in frame, type a name above, then click "Record Gesture" and hold the pose still. To improve a gesture you already trained, type its exact name and click "Add More Samples" instead — that merges new captures into the existing one rather than replacing it.

Detected gesture: none

Trained gestures are live on the page as window.MirrorMeGestures (e.g. window.MirrorMeGestures["thumbs-up"] is true/false each frame), and as a mirrorme:gesture event on window with { name, active, similarity } whenever a gesture turns on or off — open devtools console to read it, or add your own script after this one to react to it in code.

All video, segmentation and "learning" happen locally in your browser using TensorFlow.js. No frame, image or profile data is ever uploaded or sent anywhere — your profile is only saved in this browser's local storage.
